Security

Last updated 10 November 2026

Renewless reads billing emails and stores subscription details, so protecting that data is part of the product. This page explains how. For what we collect and why, see the Privacy Policy.

Gmail access

  • Renewless asks Google only for read-only access (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, change or delete mail.
  • It searches for billing emails only, and keeps receipt details and attachments, never the full email.
  • Your Gmail access token is stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) on our server and never sent to the app.
  • Disconnecting an inbox revokes the Google grant and deletes the stored token. You can also remove access from your Google account.

Your account

  • Passwords are stored only as a hash.
  • Sign-in and reset codes are stored hashed and expire after 5 minutes.
  • Session tokens are stored only as a SHA-256 hash, one session per device. A session ends after 90 days without use, or when you sign out.
  • Google and Apple sign-in tokens are verified on our server.

Files and AI

  • Receipt attachments are kept in private storage that only the Renewless server can read.
  • Uploads to AI chat are checked by their file contents, not just their name, and are deleted within 1 hour of being read.
  • Google Gemini processes billing emails and AI chat messages on a paid plan that does not use them to train AI models. AI chat asks for your consent first.

In transit and on our servers

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Every request checks that the data belongs to the signed-in account.
  • Deleting your account deletes all your data at once. Backup copies are removed within 30 days.
