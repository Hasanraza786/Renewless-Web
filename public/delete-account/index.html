Delete your Renewless account

Renewless app by Renewless

In the app

  1. Open Renewless and go to Profile.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm. You may be asked to sign in again first.

Without the app

Email info.renewless@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject Delete my account. We reply when it is done.

What is deleted

Everything, at once: your account and profile, subscriptions, receipts and attachments, payments, AI chat messages and reminders. Your Gmail access is revoked. Backup copies are removed within 30 days. After that, nothing is kept.

What is not affected

Deleting your Renewless account does not cancel any subscription with the services you pay for. Cancel those with each service.
