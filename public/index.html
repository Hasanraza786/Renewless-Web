How it works

From inbox to list in four steps

Connect Gmail once. Renewless finds your subscriptions, you keep the ones you want.

  1. Connect Gmail

    Sign in with Google and allow read-only access. Renewless cannot send or delete mail.

  2. Renewless looks

    It reads the last 12 months of billing mail and groups receipts by service.

  3. You choose

    Review what was found and keep only the subscriptions you still pay for.

  4. You're set

    Your list, calendar and reminders are ready. No Gmail? Add them yourself or tell Loop.

Privacy

Your inbox stays yours

Renewless only looks for billing emails, and only keeps what it needs.

  • Read-only Gmail

    Renewless cannot send or delete mail. It keeps receipt details and attachments, never the full email.

  • Clear about AI

    Google Gemini reads billing emails and your Loop chats only to fill in details, on a plan that does not train AI models. Loop asks before your first chat.

  • Delete means delete

    Delete your account and your data is deleted at once. Backup copies clear within 30 days.

  • You stay in control

    Renewless never cancels for you. It opens the service's own manage page.

FAQ

Questions

Is Renewless free?

Yes. Every feature in this version is free, including Gmail scans, reminders and the widget. AI chat with Loop includes 50 free messages a month.

Do I have to connect Gmail?

It's your choice. Connect Gmail and Renewless finds your subscriptions for you, or add them yourself, by hand or by telling Loop with text, voice, a photo or a PDF. You can use both, and connect Gmail later from your Profile.

Is my Gmail data safe?

Access is read-only. Renewless looks only for billing emails and keeps their receipt details and attachments, not the full email. You can disconnect an inbox at any time.

Can Renewless cancel a subscription for me?

No. It opens the service's own manage page. After you cancel there, mark it as cancelled in Renewless.

Does it work with Outlook or iCloud mail?

No, Renewless reads Gmail only. You can still add any subscription by hand or with Loop.
