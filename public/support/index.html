Common questions

  • An inbox stopped scanning: open Profile, then your connected inbox, and reconnect it.
  • A new subscription is not showing: open Profile, then your connected inbox, and tap Sync. Sync is available once a week per inbox, and the app shows when the next one opens. Renewals of subscriptions you already track are checked automatically.
  • Reminders don't arrive: check that notifications for Renewless are on in your phone's settings and in Renewless Settings.
  • A date or price is wrong: open the subscription and tap Edit.
  • Cancelling a subscription: Renewless can't cancel for you. Use the manage link on the subscription, then mark it as cancelled.
