Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

This policy explains what the Renewless app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how it is deleted. Questions: admin@renewlessapp.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address and a hashed password, or your Google, Apple or Microsoft (Outlook) sign-in identity. Optional profile details you add, such as phone number, date of birth and gender. Your time zone, so reminders arrive on the right day.
  • Subscriptions you add: service name, plan, price, billing cycle, dates, trial details and notes.
  • Gmail or Outlook, only if you connect an inbox: read-only access. Renewless searches your inbox for billing emails: the last 12 months on the first scan. After that it reads new billing emails only when you tap Sync. Around each renewal date of a subscription you track, it also checks that subscription's billing emails to confirm the payment. It reads no other email in that check. For each billing email it keeps the sender, subject, date, amount, type of email and any PDF or image attachments. It does not keep the full email. In Outlook it skips the Junk and Deleted Items folders.
  • AI chat with Loop: the messages, voice notes, images and PDFs you send. For a voice note, Renewless also keeps a text transcript of what you said.
  • Device: device ID and name, platform, app version and a notification token, so reminders reach your phone.
  • App usage and crashes: which app features are used (for example, a subscription was added or a filter was changed) and crash reports, so we can fix problems. These never include subscription names, prices, emails, notes or messages, and are not linked to your account.
  • Messages to support: what you send from Help & support › Contact us (topic and message), your name and email address, the app version, platform, OS version and phone model, and our replies.

How we use it

Only to run the features you use: tracking subscriptions and trials, finding billing emails, filling in subscription details, recording confirmed payments, sending reminders and answering your messages to support. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use your data to train AI models.

Who we share it with

Service providers that run Renewless for us, under their own data protection terms:

  • Google Cloud and Firebase: hosting, database, file storage for receipt attachments, push notifications, app usage analytics (Firebase Analytics) and crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). iPhone notifications are delivered through Apple.
  • Google Gemini: reads the relevant part of the billing emails found in an inbox scan, and what you send in AI chat, to suggest subscription details. AI chat sends nothing until you agree, and you can turn it off in Settings.
  • Microsoft (Outlook): only if you sign in with Outlook or connect an Outlook inbox. Microsoft confirms your sign-in, and Renewless reads that inbox's billing emails through Microsoft Graph.
  • Google (Gmail): sends sign-up and password-reset codes from info.renewless@gmail.com.

We may also disclose data when the law requires it.

Google user data

Renewless's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to find and show your subscriptions and payments. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to train AI models. People do not read your email, except with your permission, for security, or when the law requires it. Email from a connected Outlook inbox is handled the same way.

How long we keep it

  • Your data is kept while your account exists.
  • When you delete your account, all of it is deleted at once: account, subscriptions, receipts and attachments, payments, chat messages, messages to support and reminders. Access to your connected Gmail and Outlook inboxes is removed. Backup copies are removed within 30 days. To prevent abuse of the free inbox scan, we keep a one-way hash of each scanned inbox address (not the address itself) and the date of its last scan for one year.
  • Voice notes, images and PDFs sent in AI chat are deleted within 1 hour of being read. Chat messages, including the text transcript of each voice note, stay until you delete your account.
  • When you disconnect a Gmail inbox, scanning stops and Renewless asks Google to revoke its access. Once Google confirms, the access token is deleted. If Google does not confirm, the token stays stored encrypted and unused, so you can reconnect later; you can remove the access yourself in your Google account permissions, and it is revoked when you delete your account. Subscriptions, payments, and receipts with their attachments that were already added are kept, and are deleted with your account.
  • When you disconnect an Outlook inbox, scanning stops and Renewless deletes its access token. Microsoft does not let Renewless revoke the access itself, so you can also remove Renewless in your Microsoft account.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete any subscription in the app.
  • Disconnect an inbox in the app, or remove Renewless from your Google account permissions or your Microsoft account.
  • Turn off AI chat in Settings.
  • Turn off reminders in Settings or in your phone's notification settings.
  • Delete your account.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Inbox access tokens are stored encrypted, and receipt attachments are in private storage that only the Renewless server can read. More on the Security page.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above. Important changes are announced in the app.
