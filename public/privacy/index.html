Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

This policy explains what the Renewless app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how it is deleted. Questions: info.renewless@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address and a hashed password, or your Google or Apple sign-in identity. Optional profile details you add, such as phone number, date of birth and gender. Your time zone, so reminders arrive on the right day.
  • Subscriptions you add: service name, plan, price, billing cycle, dates, trial details and notes.
  • Gmail, only if you connect it: read-only access. Renewless searches your inbox for billing emails: the last 12 months on the first scan, then new mail. For each billing email it keeps the sender, subject, date, amount, type of email and any PDF or image attachments. It does not keep the full email.
  • AI chat with Loop: the messages, voice notes, images and PDFs you send.
  • Device: device ID and name, platform, app version and a notification token, so reminders reach your phone.

How we use it

Only to run the features you use: tracking subscriptions and trials, finding billing emails, filling in subscription details, recording confirmed payments and sending reminders. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use your data to train AI models.

Who we share it with

Service providers that run Renewless for us, under their own data protection terms:

  • Google Cloud and Firebase: hosting, database, file storage for receipt attachments, and push notifications. iPhone notifications are delivered through Apple.
  • Google Gemini: reads the relevant part of the billing emails found in a Gmail scan, and what you send in AI chat, to suggest subscription details. AI chat sends nothing until you agree, and you can turn it off in Settings.
  • Google (Gmail): sends sign-up and password-reset codes from info.renewless@gmail.com.

We may also disclose data when the law requires it.

Google user data

Renewless's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to find and show your subscriptions and payments. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to train AI models. People do not read your email, except with your permission, for security, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Your data is kept while your account exists.
  • When you delete your account, all of it is deleted at once: account, subscriptions, receipts and attachments, payments, chat messages and reminders. Your Gmail access is revoked. Backup copies are removed within 30 days.
  • Voice notes, images and PDFs sent in AI chat are deleted within 1 hour of being read.
  • When you disconnect a Gmail inbox, its access token is deleted and scanning stops. Subscriptions and payments already added stay until you delete them or your account.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete any subscription in the app.
  • Disconnect a Gmail inbox in the app, or remove Renewless from your Google account permissions.
  • Turn off AI chat in Settings.
  • Turn off reminders in Settings or in your phone's notification settings.
  • Delete your account.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Gmail access tokens are stored encrypted, and receipt attachments are in private storage that only the Renewless server can read. More on the Security page.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above. Important changes are announced in the app.
